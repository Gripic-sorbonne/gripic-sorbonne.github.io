## Expertises

 I am a PhD candidate in Communication studies at Sorbonne University, working as a Lecturer and a Consultant. Specialist of cultural and creative industries as well as digital cultures.
My doctoral research focuses on digital culture and virtual reality (XR/VR) both in France and South Korea, through an ethnographic fieldwork about VR uses in arcade rooms, festivals and museums. My other research interests deal with online communities, live-streaming practices, digital arts and webtoon.
My lectures at Sorbonne University are about digital anthropology, cross-cultural communication, cultural industries, digital ecology, semiotics, qualitative and fieldwork methodologies in social sciences...
